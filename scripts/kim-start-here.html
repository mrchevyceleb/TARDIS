YOUR OFFICE, READY TO UNPACK

Welcome to TARDIS.

This USB adds your agents and content studio to the Linux computer you already have. Keep AMD’s factory Linux and drivers.

1. Finish the computer’s welcome screens

Connect the screen, keyboard, mouse and internet. Create your own Linux account, complete AMD’s updates, and let it restart. Keep your computer password handy.

2. Install your office

Open this USB’s folder in the file manager. Right-click inside the folder and choose Open in Terminal. Enter:

bash INSTALL.sh

Enter your computer password when asked. Nothing appears while you type a Linux password; that is normal. Keep the internet connected and the USB plugged in until setup finishes.

The installer downloads packages. It does not erase the computer, change its GPU drivers or sign into anyone’s accounts. If it stops, keep the displayed message and see RECOVERY.txt.

3. Open Start here

Open TARDIS from Applications. The guided desk checks your connections, helps you make a blog and social posts, adds images and footage, and offers an optional daily drafting routine.

Use Finish TARDIS Setup from Applications for Claude, Codex and Grok sign-ins. Connect publishing accounts in Content and your other accounts in Integrations. Google OAuth application setup is required once for Gmail.

Open Start here on this computer →

4. Make your first content together

  1. Test one signed-in writer.
  2. Open Content for your morning ideas across Operly, R-Link and Kim Garst. Choose Make it to create a blog, email, social posts and video package.
  3. Review each format. Dictate quick changes and review formatted previews. Blog, email and every social post’s artwork is required; Generate required images repairs missing artwork without rewriting the draft.
  4. Once all agents and jobs are idle, reboot and check that the drafts are still there.
  5. Choose Schedule & save, review destinations and local times, then confirm the selected formats. Emails save as drafts in GoHighLevel. Finish the subject, sender, audience and sending there. Connect the brand’s GHL location and user under Publishing & settings first.

Nothing publishes automatically. Nightly research brings 15 ideas total across Operly, R-Link and Kim Garst; accepting ideas starts drafting. The separate daily drafting routine starts off. Connect fal.ai in Start here and enable Nano Banana 2 images and MiniMax H3 video. Blogs, emails and every social post generate artwork automatically and wait for it before export. Image/video generation services can have separate costs; your own media works without those subscriptions.
